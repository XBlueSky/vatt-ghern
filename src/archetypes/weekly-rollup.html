structure

Weekly-rollup is a synthesised look-back across one ISO week (Mon–Sun) of daily content. It reuses what's already in src/posts/YYYY/MM/DD/ for the previous seven days — no new reporting pass — and finds the dominant theme.

required structure

The rollup is essay-shaped; the universal contract from deep-freeform applies:

  • <p class="vg-deep-opener"> — one or two sentences that frame the week, stand-alone pull-quote shape
  • <p class="vg-deep-closer"> with a <strong> closing label near the end
  • ≥ 1 inline <svg> widget — typically a tag/topic distribution or domain heat-map for the week

title shape

第 W 週 ——〈主題短句〉 where W is the ISO week number, ≤ 28 characters including spaces. Example: 第 21 週 —— 契約的顯化.

placement on the homepage

A weekly-rollup becomes the cover of its week in the past-weeks list — replacing that week's day rows. The week label links to /weeks/<weekKey>/ where the full day list lives alongside the weekly.
